The trip's main event was at the U.S. Embassy in Gaborone, where we advocated with government ministers and business leaders for more STEM funding in schools, funding for robotics clubs, and lower tariffs on educational STEM goods. The day before, at Dare to Dream's office, a teammate and I wrote an "Introduction to FIRST and FLL" speech, and with students from a local FLL team we set up hands-on stations, each run by one of us and one of them: a robot game demo, a code-it-yourself area, an innovation project and the core values. At the event four of us gave the speech, written and memorized within a day of meeting: FIRST's technical side, its core values, and what it had done for each of us. After a panel, the dignitaries who had to leave early saw a quick robot demo, and the guests went round the stations, where the coding and robot demos drew the most interest.
