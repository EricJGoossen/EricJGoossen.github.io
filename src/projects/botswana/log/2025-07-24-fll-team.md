{# Confirmed by Eric (Oct 25). From the trip blog (day 12, Jul 24, 2025) and Eric (Oct 24): the day before the embassy event, at Dare to Dream's office, he prepared with a local FLL team and taught them some programming for the event. Photo from Eric (Oct 24). #}
{% row "images/fll-prep.jpg", "Eric and a student from the FLL team talking over a LEGO robot on a FIRST LEGO League field, with other students working behind them", { caption: "Setting up the robot demo with one of the team's students." } %}
The day before the embassy event, we prepared at Dare to Dream's office with students from a local FIRST LEGO League team. Together we set up the hands-on stations for the event, each to be run by one of us and one of them: a robot game demo, a code-it-yourself area, an innovation project and the core values. I also taught them some programming to get them ready for the event, and a teammate and I wrote the "Introduction to FIRST and FLL" speech we would give there.
