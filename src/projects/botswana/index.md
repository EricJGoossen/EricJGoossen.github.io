In July 2025 I traveled to Botswana with Swerve Robotics to help bring FIRST LEGO League to the country with our partner there, Dare to Dream, founded by Captain Kgomotso Phatsima. The trip built to an event at the U.S. Embassy in Gaborone, where we advocated with government ministers and business leaders for more STEM funding in schools, funding for robotics clubs, and lower tariffs on educational STEM goods. Before it, we ran a robotics exchange with students at a secondary school in Gaborone. Placeholder: the BTV appearance.
