Success alone also hides a lot, so in the main benchmark each rollout was scored three ways: did it succeed, what fraction of the goals were met, and how far along a written rubric it got. Separately from that benchmark, we ran an ablation on wording alone: one task with the apple held still and only the instruction changed, fifteen phrasings run once each. π0.5 succeeded on "Grab the apple and set it on the pan" and failed on "Take the apple and place it onto the pan."

Across all of it, the VLAs were smooth on familiar pick and place and brittle elsewhere: they depended on the object (π0 never got the baseball into the bucket), ignored clearance, grabbed the drawer's lip instead of pushing it, and gave up halfway through two-object tasks.
