I was on FIRST Tech Challenge team 417 SKID from May 2023 to May 2025, and captain in my second year. I ran every meeting with an agenda for each member, set the timelines that got us ready for competition, and oversaw CAD and programming, mentoring our programmers most closely. I led the robot's early development (decision matrices, prototypes, the first CAD), our outreach, and competition prep, from the engineering portfolio to presenting to the judges. I'm proudest of helping a teammate on the edge of the team find projects he loved, by working with him directly, pairing him with an older mentor and asking for his ideas in meetings.

Two of my own projects appear in the log. **Live View** (winter break 2024–25) sends the robot's camera feed to the driver station as Braille-character text, which keeps it within FTC's rules. **SKIDADDLE** is a Java path-following library that plans each path with a physics model of the robot, using math I derived by hand. I wrote it after my time on the team, at the end of summer 2025, and reworked its control in January 2026.
