V2's dynamics were wrong in ways only a reference could show: brought back to life next to MuJoCo, a one-link arm swung exactly 4× too fast (it used the free-body inverse inertia, dropping the parallel-axis term), and a two-link arm moved in the wrong shape. V3 runs the full Articulated Body Algorithm, with Coriolis and centrifugal bias terms, parent-to-body transforms and gravity as a fictitious base acceleration. On a double pendulum, its joint accelerations match the closed-form Lagrangian equations of motion, and MuJoCo, to within 3×10⁻⁶: float32 round-off. With the dynamics right, the integrator decides how long a simulation stays physical, so I built four: semi-implicit Euler (the library default, like MuJoCo's), velocity Verlet, implicit midpoint and RK4. Over 10 seconds at a 2 ms step, Euler's total energy wanders by up to 0.56 J and Verlet's by 0.28 J, while implicit midpoint stays within 0.0006 J and RK4 within 0.0001 J, at the price of four dynamics evaluations per step.
