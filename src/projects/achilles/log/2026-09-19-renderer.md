The V3 design doc's rule for rendering was to hand it to a library rather than hand-roll it, so the viewer is a small GLFW and OpenGL program. It draws only from the simulation's own `viz` state: the world transforms and visual boxes of a render-only algorithm that is never stepped. Simulation ticks and drawn frames are independent, and the `achilles` binary opens the viewer unless you pass `--headless`. Below, eight double pendulums released 0.002 rad (about 0.1°) apart: after a few seconds chaos has sent each one its own way.
