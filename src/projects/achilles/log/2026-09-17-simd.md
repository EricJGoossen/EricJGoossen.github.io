V3 stores every field as a structure of arrays: each component of each field, say the x angular velocity of every joint, is its own contiguous, aligned array across all the robots. So one instruction can load, update or store the same quantity for eight robots at once (eight lanes with AVX2; the default SSE build does four), and independent robots step in lockstep, one per SIMD lane. A Valgrind trace of the compiled binary confirms it: **88.9% of all accesses to simulation data are whole, aligned 8-lane vector loads or stores**. The payoff shows up as soon as the lanes are full: on a seven-link arm, one robot runs at 0.75 million steps per second, and 64 robots stepped together run at 5.45 million.
