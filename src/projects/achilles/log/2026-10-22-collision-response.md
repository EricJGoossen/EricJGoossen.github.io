Detecting contacts is half the job; the contact solver decides what they do. It's MuJoCo's soft-constraint solver (primal conjugate gradient, pyramidal friction cones), run in MuJoCo's own step order: ABA computes the unconstrained accelerations, the broad and narrow phases find this pose's contacts, and the solver replaces ABA's accelerations with constrained ones before the integrator uses them. To push it, I built a domino amplifier: ten dominoes, each 1.2× the size of the last, so the last weighs **137 times the first** (0.3 kg to 41 kg). Large mass ratios across a chain of contacts are a classic way to break a contact solver. Achilles topples the whole chain, and run against MuJoCo 3.15 on the same scene, **every domino tips within 56 ms of MuJoCo's time** and comes to rest at the same angle.
