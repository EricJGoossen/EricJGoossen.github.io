V2's dynamics were wrong in ways only a reference could show: brought back to life next to MuJoCo, a one-link arm swung exactly 4× too fast (it used the free-body inverse inertia, dropping the parallel-axis term), and a two-link arm moved in the wrong shape. V3 runs the full Articulated Body Algorithm, with Coriolis and centrifugal bias terms, parent-to-body transforms and gravity as a fictitious base acceleration, and steps it forward with semi-implicit Euler, the same default MuJoCo uses. On a double pendulum, its joint accelerations match the closed-form Lagrangian equations of motion to within 3×10⁻⁶, which is float32 round-off, and match MuJoCo to the same 3×10⁻⁶.
