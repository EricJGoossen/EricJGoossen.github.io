The design doc planned cuRobo-style sphere colliders. What I built is more general: every body is a sphere-swept box, so zero extents make a sphere, one extent a capsule and zero radius a plain box. A broad phase tests swept-box world bounds and writes a bitmask of candidate pairs, and a MuJoCo/MJX-style narrow phase turns each candidate into up to four contacts with a normal, a signed distance and a point, computed across SIMD lanes like everything else. Against an exact signed-distance calculation on 1,800 random sphere, capsule and box pairs, **every distance Achilles reported agreed to within 5×10⁻⁵** (median 2×10⁻⁸). MuJoCo, on the same pairs, matched for spheres and capsules, but its box colliders, which approximate some cases, were off on 86.
