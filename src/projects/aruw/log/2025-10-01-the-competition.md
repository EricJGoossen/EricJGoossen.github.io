Advanced Robotics at UW competes in ARC, the Advanced Robotics Competition: DJI's university robotics competition in North America, called RoboMaster North America until 2025. Each team builds a fleet of robots that fire small projectiles at the other team's armor plates, in 1v1 and 3v3 matches, each a best of three. The 3v3 brings in the hero and the sentry, which drives, aims and fires entirely on its own, alongside an engineer robot that picks up and delivers cubes. I've worked on two of them: the engineer's odometry and path tracking, and the sentry's localization and navigation.
