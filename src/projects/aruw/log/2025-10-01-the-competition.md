Advanced Robotics at UW competes in ARC, DJI's robotics competition for universities in North America (RoboMaster North America until 2025). Every season the team designs and builds a fleet of eight robots, most of them new, for what the team calls a deathmatch somewhere between paintball and League of Legends. Operators drive their robots through an onboard camera and fire 17 mm and 42 mm projectiles at the pressure-sensitive armor plates on the other team's robots, while fighting king-of-the-hill style for a capture point in the middle of the field, in matches that are each a best of three. The engineer can't shoot: it collects minerals to trade for coins that buy supplies during the match. The sentry is the only fully autonomous robot on the field, finding its own way around and aiming on its own. ARUW won the competition every year from 2021 to 2024. At the 2026 ARC Championship at Purdue we placed third in the 3v3, second in the dart event and third in the drone event; for it I worked on the engineer's odometry and path tracking, and since then I've moved to the sentry's localization and navigation.
