Advanced Robotics at UW competes in ARC, DJI's robotics competition for universities in North America (RoboMaster North America until 2025). Every season the team designs and builds a fleet of eight robots, most of them new, for what the team calls a deathmatch somewhere between paintball and League of Legends. Operators drive their robots through an onboard camera and fire 17 mm and 42 mm projectiles at pressure-sensitive armor plates, to take down the other team's robots and disarm its base, in 1v1 and 3v3 matches that are each a best of three. The engineer can't shoot: it collects minerals to trade for coins that buy supplies during the match. The sentry is the only fully autonomous robot on the field. It defends the base, whose shield drops if the sentry dies or leaves, finding its own way around the field and aiming on its own. ARUW won the competition every year from 2021 to 2024 and took third in all three events in 2025. For the 2026 ARC Championship at Purdue I worked on the engineer's odometry and path tracking; since then I've moved to the sentry's localization and navigation.
