From the October 3 campaign (25 figure-8 laps at about 1 m/s, a ten-minute hold, seven relocalizations and six covered-sensor trials, all against mocap), I approximated the Odin's error with a small model: the true pose, plus an offset drawn once each time it relocalizes into the map, plus noise that is correlated over time but decays.

Standing still, the noise stays around 1 mm and 0.2°. Moving, it is about 1 cm horizontally, 0.5 cm vertically and 0.3°, each error lasting about two seconds, and it stays bounded rather than accumulating. Each relocalization adds an offset with a standard deviation of 2.7 cm in x, 1.3 cm in z and 0.3°, which lasts until the next one. On a held-out session the real error, 5.1 cm horizontal, 0.6 cm vertical and 0.25–0.45°, fell within what the model predicted. 90% of the time the error is under 5 cm horizontal, 2.5 cm vertical and 1°, small enough to prevent collisions during autonomous navigation, so I recommended the Odin as the sentry's only localization, mounted on the turret major as it already was, where it can swivel independently of the turret and the chassis. The two exceptions are for the nav stack to handle: a rare wrong relocalization, and 4–6 s pauses in its output when the sensor is covered.
