The afternoon teleop first ran on the robot, I led a demo for twenty to thirty executives from Winbond, the memory chip maker, shortly before [Winbond's $12 million gift to the UW College of Engineering](https://www.washington.edu/news/2026/10/13/coengwinbond/). I prepared and ran it: the robot drove around the room while I teleoperated both arms and explained how it works.

Two days earlier, Fauna Robotics' team visited the lab, and I gave them the same tour: driving the robot around and talking through how it works.
