On October 17, OpenDubs went downtown for the [ARCS Foundation](https://seattle.arcsfoundation.org/)'s Night to Ignite Science, a fundraiser for STEM PhD fellowships at the UW and Washington State that raised over $900,000. Four of us from the lab presented it to donors. The robot danced and moved its arms to the Monster Mash: a routine others in the lab put together, running on the arm infrastructure I built.

I really enjoyed the chance to talk with and give back a little to the people who make my research at UW possible. I talked the visitors through the motivation behind OpenDubs, my work on the project, and my passion for robotics. It was great to practice pitching my work to people with an interest in the field, but without intense technical depth.
