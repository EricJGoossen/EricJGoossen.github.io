The lab plans with its own stack, built on [mj_manipulator](https://github.com/EricJGoossen/mj_manipulator), which planned one arm at a time. With two arms on one torso, two plans that are each safe can still hit each other. I rebuilt planning around an `ArmGroup` that plans all arms' joints together, so every step is checked arm against arm. On the clip's goals, the two arms planned separately collide in 58 of 210 frames; **ArmGroup's plan bends one gripper about 16 cm around the other and touches in none.**
{% endrow %}

{% row "images/hero-handover.mp4", "The two arms passing a red can between them over a table and setting it down, then passing it back", { poster: "images/hero-handover.jpg", caption: "A two-arm handover planned with ArmGroup in MuJoCo (the can is carried kinematically)." } %}
The same group plans to joint goals, gripper poses and lockstep Cartesian paths, keeps 5 mm between all geometry, and re-checks the retimed trajectory at every sample. Single-arm code is just a one-arm group, and it's what plans both arms on the robot today.
