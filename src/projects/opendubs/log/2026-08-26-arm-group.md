The lab plans with its own MuJoCo planning library, mj_manipulator, which planned one arm at a time. With two arms on one torso, two plans that are each safe can still hit each other. I added [an `ArmGroup`](https://github.com/EricJGoossen/mj_manipulator) to it that plans all arms' joints together, so every step is checked arm against arm. On the clip's goals, the two arms planned separately collide in 58 of 210 frames; **ArmGroup's plan bends one gripper about 16 cm around the other and touches in none.** The same group plans to joint goals, gripper poses and lockstep Cartesian paths, keeps 5 mm between all geometry, retimes the trajectory at 125 Hz, and re-checks it at every sample. I made single-arm planning a one-arm group, and ArmGroup is what plans both arms on the robot today.
