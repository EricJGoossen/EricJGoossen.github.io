The lab plans with its own stack, built on [mj_manipulator](https://github.com/EricJGoossen/mj_manipulator), which planned one arm at a time. With two arms on one torso, two plans that are each safe can still hit each other. I rebuilt planning around an `ArmGroup` that plans all arms' joints together, so every step is checked arm against arm. On the clip's test case, straight-line motion collides in 467 of 2,798 steps; **ArmGroup's plan has zero contacts, found in 1.4 s.**

The same group plans to joint goals, gripper poses and lockstep Cartesian paths, keeps 5 mm between all geometry, and re-checks the retimed trajectory at every sample. Single-arm code is just a one-arm group, and it's what plans both arms on the robot today.
