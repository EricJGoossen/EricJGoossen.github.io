For demonstrations and live driving, I built [teleop with a Meta Quest](https://github.com/EricJGoossen/openarm). The apps I tried had no passthrough and left me dizzy, so I wrote my own: instead of an app on the headset, the robot serves a page the Quest's browser opens in AR, so the operator still sees the room, and the page streams both controllers' poses, grip and trigger to the robot over a websocket. Holding a grip engages its arm, and the trigger works the gripper. The headset's page goes dark once it enters AR, so the robot also broadcasts that live telemetry to any other browser pointed at it, and someone at a laptop can watch exactly what the operator is sending. It matches the old SDK's interface, so switching was a one-line change. I built it against the arms in MuJoCo first; at first the loop couldn't keep up, and the bottleneck turned out to be publishing the sim viewer over the network, not the control.
