For demonstrations and live driving, I built [teleop with a Meta Quest](https://github.com/EricJGoossen/openarm). Instead of an app on the headset, the robot serves a page the Quest's browser opens in AR, streaming both controllers' poses, grip and trigger over a websocket. It matches the old SDK's interface, so switching was a one-line change. Holding a grip engages its arm, and the trigger works the gripper.
{% endrow %}

{% row "placeholder", "Simulation: the teleop loop tracking two scripted hands at the robot's raised speed limits" %}
I built it against the arms in MuJoCo first. At first the loop couldn't keep up, and the bottleneck turned out to be publishing the sim viewer over the network, not the control. The [analytical IK solver](https://github.com/EricJGoossen/ssik) also failed on the OpenArm's zero-length links; I traced and patched it, and the maintainer confirmed my fix while landing the same one upstream. Teleop first ran on the robot the morning of September 24.
